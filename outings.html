Go Farther

Outings & outdoor program

Troop 199 learns by doing. Our program ranges from local hikes and weekend camping to summer camp, aquatics, service, and challenging backcountry treks.

Our Program

Adventure for every stage of Scouting

Scouts build skills progressively: first in meetings and local outings, then on longer and more demanding adventures.

01

Camping & Hiking

Troop destinations have included Angel Island, New Brighton, Sunset State Beach, Mount Diablo, Mount Tamalpais, Mount St. Helena, Pinnacles, Mission Peak, Sunol, and Del Valle.

02

Summer Camp

Extended camps such as Wente, High Sierra, Camp Royaneh, Emerald Bay, and Camp Wolfeboro give Scouts time to develop skills, complete advancement, and strengthen patrol teamwork.

03

High Adventure

Troop experience includes ski and snowboard trips, rappelling and ziplining at Moaning Caverns, whitewater rafting, Philmont, Florida Sea Base, Grand Canyon trekking, and a 54-mile Mount Whitney trek.

04

Service Outdoors

Conservation and community projects put the Scout Law into action. Outdoor ethics are part of planning, travel, camping, and cleanup on every trip.

Before an outing

Read the trip information

Know the destination, expected weather, departure and return plan, activity level, required forms, and trip leader instructions.

Choose the right checklist

Car camping, backpacking, winter camping, and summer camp need different gear. Start with the troop list and adjust only for the outing.
