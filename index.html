About Us

A troop built on service, skill, and the outdoors

Troop 199 was formed in May 1984 and serves Fremont, California as part of the Mission Peak District of the Golden Gate Area Council. The troop is chartered by South Bay Community Church.

We follow the Scout Oath and Law through a vigorous outdoor program of hiking, camping, backpacking, and aquatics. Troop 199 strives to be Scout-run and organized, supported by active leaders and involved parents.

The Troop 199 Experience

Adventure with a purpose

Troop 199 offers a medium-to-high adventure program with a strong focus on service. Scouts build confidence by planning activities, leading patrols, learning outdoor skills, and helping the community.

01

Camping & Hiking

Past destinations include Angel Island, New Brighton and Sunset State Beaches, Mount Diablo, Mount Tamalpais, Mount St. Helena, Pinnacles, Mission Peak, Sunol, and Del Valle.

02

Summer Camp

Troop traditions include Wente, the High Sierra, Camp Royaneh, Emerald Bay, and Camp Wolfeboro — opportunities to learn skills and grow together.

03

High Adventure

The troop has pursued skiing, snowboarding, rappelling, ziplining, whitewater rafting, Philmont, Florida Sea Base, Grand Canyon trekking, and a 54-mile Mount Whitney trek.

04

Service

Scouts serve through Scouting for Food, holiday gift distribution for families in need, conservation work, and community fundraising projects.

New Scouts Welcome

Ready to join the adventure?

New scouts and families are welcome. Come to a Wednesday meeting, meet the troop, and see if Troop 199 is a fit. No prior scouting experience is required.

  • Open to scouts ages 11–17
  • Meetings every Wednesday, 7:00–8:30 PM (except holidays)
  • Warm Springs Elementary Multi Purpose Room (MUR), Fremont
  • No prior scouting experience required
